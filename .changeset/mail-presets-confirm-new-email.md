A sixteenth preset, `confirm-new-email`: a link e-mailed to the new address when a user changes the e-mail of their account, which moves only once the link is clicked, written with `@nxgt/mail-ui` components in `en` and `fr`. It takes `name`, `link` and `expiresIn`, as `verify-email`, and says that nothing changes if it was not the recipient. `PRESETS` now lists sixteen names; `only` accepts any of them.
